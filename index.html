My Journey

Understanding the career break and the valuable lessons learned

From Setback to Comeback

In 2023, I experienced a significant setback when I met with an accident that resulted in a tear to my right knee's ACL and meniscus. This injury required arthroscopic surgery followed by an extensive year-long physiotherapy regimen.

Rather than viewing this as just a career gap, I approached it as an opportunity for personal growth and skill development. During my recovery, I dedicated myself to learning new technologies, improving existing skills, and developing resilience that will benefit my professional career.

This period taught me valuable lessons in patience, perseverance, and adaptability—qualities that are essential in today's fast-paced work environment.
